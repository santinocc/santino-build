## Civil engineering, construction, and energy

I studied civil engineering at Tecnológico de Monterrey in Mexico and graduated first in my class. During the degree I spent a summer as a field engineer trainee with Schlumberger, and my first job after graduating was as a site supervisor for an architecture firm in Monterrey.

In 2015 I moved to Japan to start an M.Sc. in Earthquake Engineering at the University of Tokyo. In 2017 I transferred to the National University of Singapore, where I completed an M.Sc. in Offshore Technology with a specialization in petroleum engineering, and interned with INTECSEA on offshore pipelines.

From 2018 to 2020 I worked in construction in San Francisco: as a project engineer at Build Group, then as a senior project engineer at Guzman Construction Group.

## The move to software

After construction, I retrained as a software engineer. In 2021 I completed NuCamp's 22-week Full Stack Web & Mobile Development bootcamp (MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js, React Native), followed in 2022 by its SQL, Data Structures & Algorithms with Python course.

In November 2022 I joined JPMorgan Chase as a software engineer. Over three years I built back-end services in Java and Spring Boot, managed their AWS infrastructure in Terraform, and worked on CI/CD and observability. In 2026 I founded Chefini and built it end to end, and at ETHGlobal Tokyo 2026 I built Sapore Pay.

I'm now based in Japan.

## Interests

I speak Spanish (native) and English, and I'm studying Japanese (JLPT N4). I've lived in Mexico, Japan, Singapore, and the United States. I also enjoy cooking.
